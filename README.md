## 📌 Project Overview

The **ElectroHub Sales Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI solution designed to provide comprehensive insights into retail sales performance. The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor sales, profit, discounts, customer behavior, and product performance through dynamic visualizations and KPI tracking.

By leveraging data-driven analytics, this project helps identify revenue opportunities, optimize inventory decisions, and improve overall business performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze sales performance across products and categories.
- Identify top and bottom-performing products.
- Monitor profit and revenue trends over time.
- Evaluate discount effectiveness.
- Compare business performance between custom periods.
- Track order volume and customer purchasing behavior.
- Understand regional sales distribution.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 🏆 Product Performance Analysis
- Top 5 Products by Sales
- Top 5 Products by Profit
- Top 5 Products by Quantity Sold
- Bottom 5 Products by Sales
- Bottom 5 Products by Profit
- Bottom 5 Products by Quantity Sold

### 📅 Time-Series Analysis
- Daily Sales Trends
- Monthly Sales Trends
- Quarterly Performance Analysis
- Annual Growth Tracking

### 💰 Sales & Profit Analytics
- Sales vs Profit Relationship Visualization
- Profitability Analysis
- Revenue Trend Monitoring

### 🔄 Dynamic Period Comparison
Compare:
- Sales
- Profit
- Quantity Sold

Between any two user-selected periods.

### 🎟️ Discount Analysis
- Average Discount by Discount Category
- Discount Impact on Profitability
- Promotion Performance Evaluation

### 📦 Order Analytics
- Total Orders Overview
- Order-Level Performance Metrics
- Customer Purchase Tracking

### 🌍 Geographic Insights
- City-wise Sales Distribution
- Regional Revenue Contribution
- Location-Based Performance Analysis

### 🔍 Interactive Filtering
Users can dynamically filter dashboard data using:

- Product
- Date
- Customer ID
- Promotion Category
- City
- Discount Category

## 📊 Key KPIs

| KPI | Description |
|------|------------|
| Total Sales | Overall Revenue Generated |
| Total Profit | Business Profitability |
| Net Sales | Revenue After Discounts |
| Quantity Sold | Total Units Sold |
| Total Orders | Number of Transactions |
| Average Discount | Discount Performance |
| Profit Margin | Profitability Indicator |
